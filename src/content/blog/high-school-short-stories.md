I took a creative writing class during my senior year of high school, and it was the most interesting class my public schooling offered me. We started by writing [a 1 page short story](/blog/original), then used that story as a foundation to mimic the voices of Classics authors like [Agatha Christie](/blog/christie), [Charlotte Brontë](/blog/bronte), [J.D. Salinger](/blog/salinger), [F. Scott Fitzgerald](/blog/fitzgerald), [Harper E. Lee](/blog/lee), and [Mary Shelley](/blog/shelley). There were actually 18 total authors (a new author each week of class), but I'll spare you the cringe. 

In between graduating high school and starting engineering school, I took an English class at a local community college to save money on gen-eds. Had a lot of fun writing this [narrative short story inspired by Edgar Allan Poe's Tell-Tale Heart](/blog/tell-tale-orphan) too.
