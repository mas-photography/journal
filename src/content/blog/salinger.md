J.D. Salinger's voice, Creative Writing 2018 -- this one came way too naturally!

If you’re like everyone else, you probably won’t care about this or any of the other things I have to say. And if, for some reason, you do, you’d be the first. I can’t see the appeal, but whatever, that’s none of my business. Go ahead: keep reading if you want. I’m writing it either way because I honestly don’t give a damn. I don’t know where to start, so I’ll just tell you everything, I guess. Well, maybe not everything. You don’t want my whole life story, do you? Even if you do care about me, you can’t care that much. My life’s been pretty fucked up all throughout, so it’d be a long read. I won’t do that to you, I guess. I’ll just start with the basics: it’s been a fucking nightmare. Literally and figuratively speaking. I’ve been having these pretty much every night for a good four years and they’ve fucked me up ever since. They don’t make much sense, but at the same time, they do. It’s really weird and they make me so goddam confused all the time and I wake up even more tired than when I fall asleep. Put that on top of all the shitty people I have to deal with at my god-awful school and there you have it! The abridged edition of Mia’s Pathetic Life – sold nowhere because no one cares. Not even my doctor, if you can even call him that. Jesus, he’s a joke, and not in the good way either. I don’t know why everyone insists that I see him because our appointments are just full of his ridiculous diagnoses. No, Dr. Hett, I’m not depressed, but if you keep talking, I will be.   

God, I hate these people. They just don’t understand anything and I really don’t wanna get into it right now. I’ll just tell you about these dreams I’ve been having. They really make me question my understanding of the universe because I’m pretty sure there’s been some supernatural stuff going on. I’ve never been religious and sorry if that offends you because I do like you, especially now that you’ve made it this far, but they’re making me question my understanding of religion too. It’s like they’ve been trying to tell me something all this time and it’s really freaking me out. Random people go to my dreams and either try to talk to me or kill me and our interactions always linger in my head after I wake up and I can’t stop thinking about them. It’s like a puzzle I can’t piece together. And it’s starting to look like I’m stuck with this one for life. Puzzles are nice, I guess, but these fucked up pieces constantly morph and probably disappear too because the people that talk to me are always so damn vague. They can’t clarify what they’re trying to say to save their lives, so they just die! No rhyme or reason. They either die or leave and it’s honestly kind of annoying. These dreams truly suck because I’m pretty much wide-awake during each one. Not sure how that works, but it does. They’ll start off nice, then some whiny bitch will fucking jump me and say a few prophecy-like lines and then die and then I’d die. I’d say it’s getting old, but they always change it up a little, which is nice, I guess. I mean I still don’t understand what the hell is going on, but at least they make it interesting. But overall, I just wish they’d be straight up with me and I’m so confused and I hate them and I know you’d hate them too.
