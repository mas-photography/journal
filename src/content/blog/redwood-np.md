For a park I expected to be an endless old growth forest (because it's named after a tree), Redwood has surprising range. Fern Canyon, a 50 ft canyon covered in ferns, is a really unique landscape I haven’t seen again since I left the trail. It was dry during our visit, so couldn’t get good reflection shots on the river, but the walls were so lush and green that I wasn’t too sad about it. I also despise getting my socks wet, so maybe it was for the best. My sister requested a print of the left shot, and a coworker said these are “screensaver-worthy”. 

![Huge herd of elk next to some person's house, August 2025](/journal/elk_pasture.jpg)

We’ve been to Yellowstone before (national park with the largest elk population), and we definitely didn’t see herds this big. This was a residential area between the Redwood State Parks surrounded by houses. Wish I took a video because that elk in the middle of the photo with a grass on its head was screaming, but I didn’t know how to take videos on my camera yet, and that was *not* the time to learn. I hid behind a tree to get this shot and snuck off as the elk screams got louder. My mom requested a print of this shot and asked me to hang it up in her kitchen. Another good shot that was worth the risk!

![Gorgeous Cali coast, August 2025](/journal/gorgeous_cali_coast.jpg)

I read about the area of this park on the coastline, but I did not expect it to look this good. Maybe I'm just getting too used to the brown water down in Houston. The blue layering in the water, mountains, and sky make me want to turn this into a postcard. 

![The seal that trolled me, August 2025](/journal/trolling_seal.jpg)

I won’t lie, I thought they were sharks at first. It was hard to get a good look because the seals kept going back under water after mere seconds, and these little rascals timed it so they were making their way back down as soon as I got my camera ready. That went on for 10 minutes. This was the best seal shot I got, and it’s pretty crisp but was shot on a landscape lens (70 mm max zoom), and I didn’t have a photo editor yet, so there wasn't a good way to crop it tighter. Once I put my camera back in my bag, one of the seals came up and looked at me for at least 30 seconds straight. I’ve never felt more betrayed in my life…

I remember fighting Landon a lot about this hike because “I’ve seen enough prairies in Kansas…” but I’m glad we went anyway because this ended up being my favorite. Turns out prairies in California have gorgeous rolling hills that look blue in the distance, so this was higher elevation than most of the park and gave us our mountain fix. Thankfully, I learned how to edit shadows at this point, so these photos turned out well! 

![Wanted to tell a bridge joke but I couldn't get over it, August 2025](/journal/broken_bridge.jpg)

The turn-back point of the hike was technically at Redwood Creek just past this bridge, but we cut our losses... I was pissed, but it was still a good hike.

This is the last landscape I photographed that isn’t explicitly Redwood forests. I will never tire of the blue/teal waters out west, but that wasn’t enough to convince me to walk around in it with Landon. We were only halfway done with our hike at this point, so we still had ~7 miles to go after playing in the water, and I did not want to hike in wet socks. I admired the water with my eyes and camera, but Landon had a good time splashing around and cooling down his feet.

Last, but not least: the tallest trees in the world™. Hiking through Redwood forests was a surprisingly unique experience for hikers as well-traveled as us. Redwoods are so tall and densely packed, they completely block out wind on the trail. It was really peaceful, and the air was so fresh. Maybe it’s good for your skin too because I was told I had “that camping glow about me” when I got back to work. 

![Landon + an old, tall, thick tree, August 2025](/journal/big_tree.jpg)

Our lives are such a brief patch of time compared to these trees.

![Certified tree hugger, August 2025](/journal/tree_hugger.jpg)

Redwood National & State Parks did not disappoint <3
