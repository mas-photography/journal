Original short story, Creative Writing 2018

“Mia,” the withered woman rasped. “Save yourself.” 

As Mia tried to back away, the old woman gripped her hands with a surge of deceptive strength. She took a breath to say more, but the ground split between them and broke her grip. The ground beneath her crumbled away and Mia fell down with it. When she scrambled to hold onto something, the old woman reached out and grabbed her wrist. But as Mia started pulling herself back up, the old woman lost her grip, and left Mia to fall. So the old woman jumped to fall down with her. 	

They landed in front of an entrance with a sign that read “work will make you free.” “Mia, we don’t have much time. So I need you to listen,” the woman said apprehensively. “There’s a secret trapdoor in your school’s library. Use this key to”—the ground trembled underneath them, and started crumbling. Everything faded from view, and Mia tumbled back into the dark abyss. 

Soon enough, the darkness was replaced with light, and Mia stood in the library of her boarding school. Everything seemed normal until a group of corpselike people dressed in stripes burst through the entrance doors and ran towards her. “Help us, Mia,” they whispered. “Save us.” They all crowded around Mia and grabbed her, each trying to keep her attention. One shook her by the shoulders and cried, “Free us!” From the back of the crowd, the old woman shoved her way to the front and handed Mia a key. “Go.” she said. “Before it’s too late.” She gently pushed Mia back, and as Mia stumbled, she clutched the key to her chest.   

The crowd and the library disintegrated from view and Mia was thrown into the black abyss yet again. While she was in free fall, Mia heard them chanting her name and telling her to wake up. “Mia. Mia, wake up. Mia, wake up! It’s time for school.” Her roommate shook her and put a muffin and a glass of chocolate milk on Mia’s dresser. 
Mia woke with a throbbing headache, and vaguely remembered seeing her aunt. She rubbed her eyes and groaned. “No more caffeine,” she said to herself. As she stretched and yawned, she noticed her right hand was cramping, and clenched into a tight fist. When she opened it, she found a key pressed to the palm of her hand. Thoroughly confused, she tucked it into her back pocket and got ready for school.
