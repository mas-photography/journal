Agatha Christie's voice, Creative Writing 2018

Mia sat in a chair across her own reflection. It had been staring at her intently as she did her homework and mimicked her every move, as she expected it to. 

It waited until Mia turned away to finally step out of the mirror. And when Mia turned back, it was gone. 

Mia didn’t notice at first because her attention wasn’t on the mirror. She had been thinking about the dream she had had the night before. It was oddly coherent – so much like the others she had been having recently – and too much like real life. Her half finished math assignment lay before her and she mentally put it aside to make room for these thoughts. There was this old woman who kept coming back. She would always say something to Mia and then throw her down a cliff. And now that she thought about it, the old woman was the last part of every dream. She would always say the same things too. ‘Save yourself’ or something of that manner. Ironically, she was usually the one that killed Mia in those dreams, which was another weird thing because her dreams pretty much confirmed the legitimacy of “Inception”, in the sense that when she died in her dreams, she never died in real li –  

The new reflection tapped the surface of the mirror and said:
 “Mia, we don’t have much time. Look up.”

Mia looked into her mirror and yelled when she saw the reflection of the old woman staring back at her.
